Deep-layer photon count (I_deep) for the guanine concentration series
========================================================================================================

I_deep = (X_max - X_min)/10 * sum of the 216-point deep auxiliary head,
computed with best_raman2_model.pt on Spectrum/guanine_concentrations/*_LaserRemoved.

'paper' is the value Figure 10(b) of the manuscript reports at the same
concentration (conc_vs_photons in paper_validate_guanine.py).

The published series and these spectra are different acquisitions, so the two
columns are not expected to agree in absolute terms; the informative comparison
is the shape of the response, given at the end.
