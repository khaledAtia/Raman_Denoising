Deep-layer photon count (I_deep) for the measured guanine spectra
====================================================================================================

I_deep = (X_max - X_min)/10 * sum of the 216-point deep auxiliary head,
computed with best_raman2_model.pt on Spectrum/Guanine/100mM_LaserRemoved.

'paper' is the value Figure 10(d) of the manuscript reports at the same
acquisition time, as supplied by the authors.
Refitting those points gives I(t) = 16788 t + 178181, R^2 = 0.9935,
matching the R^2 = 0.9935 printed on the figure.

NOTE: the published series and these spectra are different acquisitions of
100 mM guanine, so the two columns are not expected to agree exactly.
